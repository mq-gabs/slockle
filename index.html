    How to play

    Crack today's three hidden unlock patterns. Each lock gives you 6 attempts.

    • Drag across the dots to draw a pattern. Dots are used once, and a line passing over an unused dot picks it up.
    • Order matters: 1→2→5 and 5→2→1 are different patterns.
    • You know the number of dots. A guess of the wrong length doesn't cost an attempt.
    • After each miss you get a new hint, and they get more specific as you go.

    Example

    The answer is 1→2→5→8→9 and you draw 1→5→9→8→7:

    • 1 and 8 are at the right step
    • 5 and 9 are in the pattern, at another step
    • 7 is not in the pattern

    A new puzzle arrives at midnight. Everyone gets the same locks. Missed a day? Play it from the calendar button.
